Frequently Asked Questions

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. We ship to the EU (7-10 working days, GBP 9.95) and the rest of the world (10-14 working days, GBP 14.95).

Can I track my parcel?

Yes, a tracking link is emailed when your order ships. Royal Mail, DPD or Evri tracking can take 24 hours to update.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. Payments are processed by Stripe and we never store full card details.

Can I change my order?

Orders can be edited for 1 hour in your account, and cancelled or re-addressed while the status is Processing.
